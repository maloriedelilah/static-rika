M. D. Cooper writes hard-edged military science fiction and space opera set in the Aeon 14 universe -- humanity's imagined future history, from first colonization through generations of war, AI, and expansion among the stars. Rika's Marauders is one corner of that universe: a mercenary company fighting to survive the Orion War, and one soldier's fight to hold onto her humanity after it was taken from her.
